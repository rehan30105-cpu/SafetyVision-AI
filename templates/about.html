Project Mission & Innovation

SAFETYVISION AI is an enterprise-grade occupational safety intelligence platform engineered to eradicate preventable workplace injuries in high-hazard industrial environments.

Conventional vision applications stop at naive object detection. SafetyVision AI introduces a multi-tier reasoning pipeline that unifies real-time multi-person tracking, anatomical spatial PPE containment, environmental proximity hazard intelligence (machinery, vehicles, safety cones), temporal persistence debouncing, and automated regulatory audit certification.

Core Technical Innovations:

  • Anatomical Spatial Association: Dynamic containment filtering correlating detected gear (Hardhat, Mask, Vest) with anatomical sub-regions of tracked human bounding boxes without requiring biometric face identification.
  • Temporal Persistence Confirmation: Eliminates single-frame false alarms and optical flickering via multi-frame temporal validation state machines.
  • Context-Aware Dynamic Risk Engine: Formulates real-time 0-100 hazard risk by compounding missing safety gear with proximity to active machinery and moving industrial vehicles.
  • Autonomous Audit & PDF Reporting: One-click shift audit sessions that compile verifiable quantitative safety telemetry into executive PDF reports.
