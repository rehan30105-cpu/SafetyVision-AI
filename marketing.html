How it works

From site footage to safety insight, in four steps

Every session runs the same verified pipeline — no black-box results, no stored biometrics.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Upload an image or video, or start a live webcam / camera session straight from the browser.

  2. 02

    Detect

    YOLOv8n locates workers and checks hardhat, mask, and vest per person with spatial association.

  3. 03

    Review

    Violations are flagged with a 0–100 dynamic risk score and zone context for fast triage.

  4. 04

    Report

    Auto-generated PDF reports and a 5-stage incident lifecycle from detection to resolution.
